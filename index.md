# Privacy Policy — Merge & Sort: Fruit Tray Drop

**Last updated:** 2026-09-19

This policy covers the Android app **Merge & Sort: Fruit Tray Drop** (package
`tech.marava.merge_sort_tray`).

## The short version

You never need an account and we never ask for your name or email. The campaign, Daily and
Endless modes work entirely offline. If you play Head-to-Head, the app talks to our own
server to run the match. Usage and crash data go to Google (Firebase). We do not sell your
data. The app shows no ads today.

## What the app collects

### Our game server (merge-sort-tray-backend.marava.tech) — Head-to-Head only

When you open Head-to-Head and the device is online, the app contacts our server over
HTTPS. It receives:

- A random guest ID the app generates on first use (not your Android ID, not your
  advertising ID). It is used to recognise the same install across matches.
- The display name you choose. **It is shown to your opponents.**
- Match data: which match you are in, the moves you make, and the result.
- Matchmaking and match codes: while you search for a random opponent, or hold a match code
  you created, your guest ID is kept temporarily (seconds for a search, up to 24 hours for an
  unused code) so we can pair you. A code works once; nothing else about it is stored.
- A Firebase Cloud Messaging push token, so we can notify you when it is your turn.

Used only to run matches and send turn notifications. Nothing from the single-player
campaign, Daily or Endless is sent to this server.

### Google Analytics for Firebase — how the game is played

- Events such as: app opened, session started and ended, a level started, completed or
  failed and why (time out, overflow, out of drops), which mode (campaign, daily,
  endless), stars earned, drops used, and whether the free continue was used.
- Automatically collected by the SDK: app version, device model, operating system
  version, language, and a country estimated from your IP address.
- A randomly generated app instance identifier. It is not your name, your email or your
  device's advertising ID, and it is reset if you clear app data or reinstall.

We use this only to understand which levels are too hard, where players stop playing, and
whether the game is worth continuing to work on.

### Firebase Crashlytics — why the game crashed

- Crash and error reports: stack trace, device model, OS version, available memory, and
  the level you were on when it happened.

Used only to fix crashes.

### Advertising

The app contains no advertising SDK and shows no ads. If that changes, this policy will be
updated and re-published before the version with ads is released.

## What stays on your device

Your settings (sound, vibration, tilt), campaign progress and stars, daily streak, endless
best, and high score are stored only on your device. They are not backed up to any server;
uninstalling the app deletes them.

## Purchases

There are no in-app purchases. If any are added they will be processed by Google Play
Billing and we will never see or store your payment details.

## Children

This app is a general-audience puzzle game and is **not directed to children under 13**.
We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has
provided personal information through this app (for example a display name), contact us
and we will delete it.

> **Decision owed before submission:** the Play Console "Target audience and content"
> declaration must match this paragraph. If the app is declared as appealing to children,
> Google Play Families policy applies and this section must be rewritten.

## Your choices

- **Stop analytics and crash collection:** uninstall the app. There is no in-app toggle.
- **Delete local data:** clear the app's data or uninstall.
- **Delete server data (display name, matches, push token):** email the address below with
  your display name. We will also request deletion of the associated analytics and crash
  data.

> **Not implemented yet:** an in-app "Delete my data" button for Head-to-Head profiles.
> Google Play requires one when an app lets users create an account-like profile. Launch
> blocker for the Head-to-Head feature.

## Region-specific rights

If you are in the EEA, the UK, or a US state with a comprehensive privacy law, you have
rights to access, correct and delete personal data about you, and to object to processing.
Exercise them by emailing the address below.

## Data retention

Server match data and profiles are kept while the profile exists and deleted on request.
Firebase Analytics event data is retained for 14 months. Crash reports are retained for
90 days.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date changes with it and the new version is
published at the same URL.
